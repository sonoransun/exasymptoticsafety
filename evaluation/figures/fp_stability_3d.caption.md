# Fixed-point stability (3D)

![Fixed-point stability (3D)](./fp_stability_3d.png)

Zoomed view of the Reuter NGFP with eigenvector arrows. Blue = relevant (`Re theta > 0`), orange = irrelevant. Arrow thickness and alpha are proportional to `|Re theta|`; dashed arcs at the tips of complex-conjugate pairs indicate spiral flow. Each arrow tip carries the numeric value of `theta_i`.

## References

- Reuter (1998), Phys. Rev. D 57, 971 [hep-th/9605030].
- Lauscher & Reuter (2002), Phys. Rev. D 65, 025013 [hep-th/0108040].
- Bonanno et al. (2020), Front. Phys. 8, 269 [2004.06810].
