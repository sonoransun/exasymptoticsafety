# NGFP versus matter content

![NGFP versus matter content](./matter_continuation.png)

Continuation of the NGFP coordinates `g*`, `lambda*` and the real parts of the critical exponents as the number of minimally coupled scalars `N_s` is varied in the EH+matter truncation. The shaded region beyond `N_s = 12` marks the foliated bound from Korver, Saueressig & Wang (2024).

## References

- Dona, Eichhorn & Percacci (2014), Phys. Rev. D 89, 084035 [1311.2898].
- Eichhorn & Schiffer (2022), in *Handbook of Quantum Gravity* [2212.07456].
- Korver, Saueressig & Wang (2024), Phys. Lett. B 855, 138789 [2402.01260].
