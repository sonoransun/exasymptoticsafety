# Annotated Einstein–Hilbert phase portrait

![Annotated Einstein–Hilbert phase portrait](./eh_phase_portrait.png)

RG flow in the (g, lambda) coupling plane (d = 4, Litim regulator). The non-Gaussian UV fixed point (NGFP, green star) at g* ~ 0.69, lambda* ~ 0.14 has two relevant directions (blue eigenvector arrows annotated with the numerical values of theta_i) and is the asymptotic-safety candidate of Reuter (1998). The Gaussian fixed point (GFP, white circle) sits at the origin. The dashed line marks the lambda = 1/2 propagator-pole singularity. The thick red curve is the back-integrated UV-critical separatrix.

## References

- Reuter (1998), Phys. Rev. D 57, 971 [hep-th/9605030].
- Lauscher & Reuter (2002), Phys. Rev. D 65, 025013 [hep-th/0108040].
- Litim (2001), Phys. Rev. D 64, 105007 [hep-th/0103195].
