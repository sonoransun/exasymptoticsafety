# The Wetterich equation

![The Wetterich equation](./wetterich_equation.png)

Schematic of the exact one-loop functional RG equation. The circle represents the trace over the full regularised propagator `(Gamma_k^(2) + R_k)^{-1}`, with a single regulator insertion `d_t R_k` (cross). Only modes near the running scale `k` contribute to the flow.

## References

- Wetterich (1993), Phys. Lett. B 301, 90.
- Reuter (1998), Phys. Rev. D 57, 971 [hep-th/9605030].
