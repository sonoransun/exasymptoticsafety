# Fixed-point stability and predictivity

![Fixed-point stability and predictivity](./fp_stability_concept.png)

Linearised RG flow near a non-Gaussian fixed point. Blue eigenvectors are relevant directions (`Re theta > 0`, UV-attractive); orange are irrelevant. The shaded strip is the UV-critical surface. The number of relevant directions equals the number of free parameters of the asymptotically safe theory.
