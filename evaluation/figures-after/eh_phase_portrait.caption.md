# Annotated Einstein–Hilbert phase portrait

![Annotated Einstein–Hilbert phase portrait](./eh_phase_portrait.png)

RG flow in the (g, lambda) coupling plane (d = 4, Litim regulator). The non-Gaussian UV fixed point (NGFP, green star) at g* ~ 0.707, lambda* ~ 0.193 has two relevant directions with the complex-conjugate critical-exponent pair theta = 1.475 ± 3.043i (blue eigenvector arrows, annotated with the theta value), so the streamlines spiral into the fixed point — the asymptotic-safety candidate of Reuter (1998). The Gaussian fixed point (GFP, white circle) sits at the origin. The crimson curve emanating from the NGFP is the back-integrated UV-critical separatrix (its outer windings blend into the colored trajectory bundle). The lambda = 1/2 propagator pole lies outside the plotted window (lambda <= 0.45).
