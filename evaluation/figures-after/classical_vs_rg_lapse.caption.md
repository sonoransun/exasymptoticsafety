# Classical vs RG-improved lapse for several masses

![Classical vs RG-improved lapse for several masses](./classical_vs_rg_lapse.png)

Overlay of the classical Schwarzschild lapse `1 - 2 G_N M / r` (dashed) and the RG-improved lapse (solid) for `M = 3, 6, 9` on a trajectory with `G_N = 0.02` (`M_crit ~ 5.7`). The sub-critical `M = 3` curve stays positive at all radii — a horizonless naked-mass remnant — while its classical counterpart still crosses zero; `M = 6`, just above critical, dips barely below zero (nearly merged inner/outer horizon pair); `M = 9` shows two well-separated horizons. At small `r` the RG-improved lapse always lies above the classical one (`G(r) <= G_N`) and rejoins it in the IR.
