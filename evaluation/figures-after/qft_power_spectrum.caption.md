# QFT power spectrum of the running Newton coupling

![QFT power spectrum of the running Newton coupling](./qft_power_spectrum.png)

Quantum-Fourier transform amplitude spectrum of g(t) along an EH trajectory, with dominant peaks highlighted. The peak frequencies correspond to the imaginary parts of the complex critical exponents at the Reuter NGFP.

## References
