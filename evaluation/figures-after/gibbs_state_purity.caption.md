# Gibbs-state purity vs inverse temperature

![Gibbs-state purity vs inverse temperature](./gibbs_state_purity.png)

Purity `Tr(rho^2)` of the diagonal Gibbs state of the scalar Laplacian on `S^4` as a function of inverse temperature `beta`. Crosses over from the maximally-mixed floor `1/d_eff` (high T) to the pure ground state (low T).
