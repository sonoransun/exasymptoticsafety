# Hawking temperature: classical vs asymptotic safety

![Hawking temperature: classical vs asymptotic safety](./hawking_temperature.png)

Hawking temperature `T_H(M) = |f'(r_+)| / (4 pi)` versus ADM mass on a trajectory with `G_N = 0.02` (`M_crit ~ 5.7`). At large `M` the RG-improved temperature (solid) tracks the classical `1/(8 pi G_N M)` curve (dashed); as `M` decreases it instead peaks near `~1.3 M_crit` (star) and then turns over, falling toward the extremal limit `T_H -> 0` as the two horizons merge at `M_crit` (dotted vertical line; cold remnant). No temperature is drawn below `M_crit`, where no horizon exists — in contrast with the classical divergence as `M -> 0`.
