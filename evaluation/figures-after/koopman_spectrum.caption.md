# Koopman spectrum (EDMD)

![Koopman spectrum (EDMD)](./koopman_spectrum.png)

Eigenvalues of the Koopman operator computed from EH trajectories via Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition with a degree-4 monomial dictionary. Eigenvalues outside the unit circle correspond to growing observables (relevant directions); inside, decaying observables (irrelevant).
