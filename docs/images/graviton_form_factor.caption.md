# Graviton form factor f(p^2)

![Graviton form factor f(p^2)](./graviton_form_factor.png)

The momentum-dependent dressing f(p^2)=G_N/G(p^2) and the running G(p^2)/G_N. In the infrared f->1 (Newtonian limit); at the fixed point f ~ p^2 so G ~ 1/p^2 (ultraviolet softening).
