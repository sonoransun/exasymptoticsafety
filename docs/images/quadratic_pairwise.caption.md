# Quadratic gravity: pairwise phase portraits

![Quadratic gravity: pairwise phase portraits](./quadratic_pairwise.png)

Six pairwise streamplots projecting the quadratic-gravity `(g, lambda, alpha, beta)` flow onto every pair of coupling axes. The frozen couplings are anchored at the NGFP. The panels containing `beta` exhibit asymptotic freedom of the Weyl-squared coupling at the GFP (Codello et al. 2009).

## References

- Codello, Percacci & Rahmede (2009), Ann. Phys. 324, 414 [0812.0785].
