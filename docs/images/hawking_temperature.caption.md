# Hawking temperature: classical vs asymptotic safety

![Hawking temperature: classical vs asymptotic safety](./hawking_temperature.png)

Hawking temperature `T_H(M) = f'(r_+)/(4 pi)` versus ADM mass. The asymptotic-safety prediction (solid blue) peaks and then drops to zero at `M_crit`, in stark contrast with the classical `1/(8 pi M)` divergence (dashed) as M shrinks.
