# RG-improved Schwarzschild lapse with horizons

![RG-improved Schwarzschild lapse with horizons](./lapse_with_horizons.png)

Lapse `f(r)` for a super-critical mass on the Bonanno-Reuter geometry, with both Cauchy (`r_-`) and event (`r_+`) horizons annotated. The de Sitter core `f -> 1` as `r -> 0` is the asymptotic-safety regularisation of the central singularity.

## References

- Bonanno & Reuter (2000), Phys. Rev. D 62, 043008 [hep-th/0002196].
- Platania (2023), in *Handbook of Quantum Gravity* [2302.04272].
